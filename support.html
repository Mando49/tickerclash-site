Frequently Asked

Is TickerClash free to play?

Yes. Core gameplay — leagues, 1v1 matches, drafts, scoring — is completely free. TickerClash Pro ($4.99/month) removes ads and increases activity limits. You can also tip the developer through the optional Tip Jar.

Is this real stock trading?

No. TickerClash is a fantasy competition game. No real money is invested, no real securities are bought or sold. We use real stock ticker performance as the competitive data, the same way fantasy football uses real player stats.

Is TickerClash gambling?

No. There are no wagers, no cash prize pools, and no chance-based mechanics. Winners are determined by the percentage performance of their picks — a function of research and skill, not luck.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. On iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → TickerClash Pro → Cancel. On Android: Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TickerClash Pro → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I get a refund?

All refunds are processed through Apple or Google, not by us. On iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play refund request flow within your Order History.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Account tab → Delete Account at the bottom of the screen. This permanently removes your profile, gameplay history, and personal data from our active systems within 30 days.

Why was my match or league scored this way?

TickerClash uses Simple % scoring: each player's score is the average percentage change of their stock picks over the match window. For short positions, the sign is inverted (a stock that drops 3% scores as +3%). Starting balances are a visual anchor; the winner is whoever has the higher average percentage change.

I found a bug. What should I include when I report it?

Email support@tickerclash.com with: device model, iOS/Android version, app version (shown at the bottom of the Account screen), what you were doing when the bug occurred, and a screenshot if possible. The more specific, the faster we can fix it.

When are the markets open?

Stock prices in TickerClash update during U.S. market hours: Monday–Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Time. Prices are frozen outside those hours and on market holidays. Matches scheduled across closed periods will resume scoring when the market reopens.
