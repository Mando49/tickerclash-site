Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TickerClash ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the TickerClash mobile application (the "App"). We believe in collecting the minimum data necessary to run a great fantasy stock trading competition — nothing more.

01Information We Collect

Account
Username, email address, hashed password, optional profile photo URL
Gameplay
League membership, draft picks, match history, scoring data, ranking points
Device
Device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, anonymized analytics identifiers
Subscription
Subscription status and entitlements (managed via RevenueCat). We do not receive or store your payment card information — all payments are processed by Apple or Google.
Communications
Feedback you send us, support inquiries, and records of our replies

We do not collect: real names (unless you add one to your profile), precise location data, contacts, photos, microphone data, health data, or financial account information.

02How We Use Information

  • Provide the App. Authenticate your account, run leagues and matches, calculate scores, display leaderboards, deliver matchmaking
  • Improve the App. Diagnose crashes, understand which features are used, prioritize development
  • Communicate with you. Send important service notices (security alerts, Terms changes, subscription receipts). We will not send promotional emails without your opt-in consent.
  • Enforce our Terms. Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Comply with law. Respond to valid legal requests, enforce our rights, protect our users

03Service Providers

We share limited data with the following service providers, who are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, cloud functions, crash reporting, push notifications
  • RevenueCat — Subscription entitlement management
  • Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) — Stock price data (we send ticker symbols only; no user data is shared)
  • Google AdMob — Advertising in the free tier (see Section 5)
  • Apple & Google Play — App distribution and payment processing

04What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not rent or trade your personal information for marketing purposes.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not build advertising profiles based on your activity.

05Advertising (Free Tier)

Users on the free tier see banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) to serve ads. You can limit ad tracking through your device settings at any time.

Upgrading to TickerClash Pro removes all advertisements and prevents AdMob from loading within the App.

06Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides encryption at rest and in transit. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms — we never see your actual password. We implement Firebase Security Rules to ensure users can only access data they are authorized to view.

No system is perfectly secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a data breach that affects your information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

07Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we will remove your personal information from active systems within 30 days, subject to the following exceptions:

  • Anonymized gameplay and analytics data may be retained indefinitely
  • Information we are legally required to retain (e.g., tax records related to subscriptions) will be kept for the required period
  • Backup archives may contain your data for up to 90 days after deletion before being overwritten

08Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access. View your account information at any time through the App's Account screen
  • Correct. Update inaccurate information through the App's Settings
  • Delete. Delete your account and associated data via Account → Delete Account
  • Export. Request a copy of your personal data by emailing support@tickerclash.com
  • Opt out. Unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To exercise these rights, contact support@tickerclash.com.

EU/UK residents have additional rights under GDPR, including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

09Children's Privacy

TickerClash is not directed to children under 17, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact support@tickerclash.com and we will delete the information promptly.

10International Users

The App is operated from the United States. If you access the App from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by email at least 14 days before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

12Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy? Reach us at support@tickerclash.com.
