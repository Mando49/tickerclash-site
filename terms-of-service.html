TickerClash is a skill-based fantasy competition game in which players build virtual portfolios using real publicly traded stock tickers as the competitive data layer. Players compete head-to-head and in leagues based on the percentage performance of their picks over defined time windows.

04No Gambling, No Investment Advice

TickerClash is a fantasy competition product, structurally similar to fantasy sports. We want to be clear about what this means:

  • No real-money stakes. Competition entry is free or covered by your subscription. There are no cash prize pools, no wagers placed on outcomes, and no payouts based on match or league results.
  • No real securities traded. All positions are simulated using virtual portfolios with a fixed starting balance. The App does not execute, facilitate, or custody any real-world trades.
  • Skill-based outcomes. Winners are determined by the percentage performance of their selections, a function of player research and judgment. The App contains no chance-based mechanics such as randomized outcomes, slot-machine-style features, or prize draws.
  • Not investment advice. Nothing in the App — including leaderboards, trends, ticker lists, or user picks — constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Do not make real-world investment decisions based on App content. Consult a licensed professional before investing real money.
